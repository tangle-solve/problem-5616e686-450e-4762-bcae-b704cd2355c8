### ALGO-000189: QMA versus QCMA
- **Subfield:** Quantum complexity and locally decodable codes
- **Open:** Determine whether polynomial-size quantum witnesses provide more verification power than polynomial-size classical witnesses checked by a quantum verifier.
- **Status:** Oracle separations are known, but the unrelativized equality or separation remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Clarifies whether quantum information itself is essential in efficiently verifiable certificates.

- **Related:** Quantum information—classical descriptions versus quantum states.
